Common questions

Health permissions: You can change Apple Health access at any time in the Health app under Sharing > Apps > Forge. Forge shows honest per-permission status in Profile > Health Permissions.

Subscriptions: Forge Pro subscriptions are managed by Apple. You can view, change, or cancel in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

Data deletion: You can export or delete locally stored Forge data from Settings inside the app. Deleting Forge data does not delete source records from Apple Health.
